ODÁRA Beauty Essentials — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

ODÁRA Beauty Essentials (“ODÁRA,” “we,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains the information we collect when you book an appointment or communicate with us.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, appointment details, service preferences, and information you choose to provide for your appointment.

How we use information

We use this information to schedule and manage appointments, process deposits, provide requested services, send appointment-related communications when you have opted in, respond to questions, and maintain business records.

SMS and WhatsApp privacy

If you separately opt in, ODÁRA may send appointment confirmations, reminders, and other message categories you specifically authorize. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact ODÁRA through its booking page or Instagram @odarabeautyes.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We may share information with service providers that help us deliver appointment messaging, payment processing, booking, and business operations, solely to provide those services. We do not sell mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent data.

Data protection and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. We retain information as needed to manage appointments, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain business records.

Your choices

Providing consent to receive messages is optional and is not a condition of booking or purchasing services. You may withdraw message consent at any time by replying STOP to SMS messages or contacting ODÁRA. You may request correction of your contact details through our booking page or Instagram @odarabeautyes.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent through the ODÁRA booking page or Instagram @odarabeautyes.
