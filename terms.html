ODÁRA Beauty Essentials — Messaging Terms

Effective date: November 9, 2026

By checking an optional messaging consent box on an ODÁRA booking form, you agree to receive the message category described next to that checkbox at the phone number you provide.

Message types and frequency

Appointment messages may include booking confirmations, appointment reminders, and appointment-related updates. If you separately opt in to post-visit follow-up, you may receive a thank-you message and an optional review request. Message frequency varies based on your appointments and selections.

Consent and opt-out

Consent to receive SMS or WhatsApp messages is not a condition of booking or purchasing any service. You may opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact ODÁRA through its booking page or Instagram @odarabeautyes. You may withdraw WhatsApp consent by contacting ODÁRA.

Charges and delivery

Message and data rates may apply. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery depends on your carrier, device, network, and the messaging provider.

Privacy

We use your contact information to provide the communications you requested. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy.

Contact

For questions, contact ODÁRA through its booking page or Instagram @odarabeautyes.
